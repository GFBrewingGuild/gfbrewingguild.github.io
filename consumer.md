# Consumers

This section is for people looking for 100% naturally gluten-free beer, breweries, retailers, taprooms, events, and practical information about finding safe and enjoyable beer.

GFBG focuses on beer made with naturally gluten-free ingredients. We do not treat gluten-reduced or gluten-removed barley beer as equivalent to naturally gluten-free beer.
