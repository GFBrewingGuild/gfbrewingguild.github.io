The following list of breweries are focused on making gluten-free beer (versus "mixed-use" breweries that primarily brew gluten-containing beers with a gluten free offering or two). For the most part these are dedicated gluten free facilities with no risk of gluten cross contamination.

'''See also:'''
* [https://www.bestglutenfreebeers.com/ BestGlutenFreeBeers.com] maintains an extensive list of gluten free beer brands in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia. (Note the site also includes [[Gluten Reduced vs Gluten Free|gluten reduced]] beers, with clear indication of which category they fall into).
* [https://www.findmeglutenfree.com/local/collections/5586036168458240 Breweries Collection] on Find Me Gluten Free
